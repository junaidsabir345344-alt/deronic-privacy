Delete your Deronic Throne account

In the game

  1. Open Deronic Throne and tap ACCOUNT on the main menu.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Type your password and tap DELETE PERMANENTLY.

By email

If you no longer have the game, email jaayfee06@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your email address, your sign-in details, and your cloud save (progress, Glory and cosmetics) are permanently deleted. Progress saved on your own phone stays until you uninstall the game. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's terms.
