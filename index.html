Deronic Throne - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Who we are

Deronic Throne ("the game") is published by Jaayfee Production. Contact: Junaidsabir345344@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your settings (graphics quality, sound volumes, button layout, language), your player name, your progress (levels, favourite heroes, recently played heroes) and match history in a local file on your device. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it.

Online matches

Online games connect players directly to each other (one player hosts, the others join with a code or address). While you play online:

  • the other players in the match can see your player name, hero, in-match actions and chat messages;
  • the host and the other players' devices can see your network (IP) address, because that is how a direct connection works;
  • if you use voice chat, your voice is sent to the other players in the match while you hold or enable it. It is not recorded or stored by us.

Only join online games with people you are comfortable sharing this information with. You can play offline against bots at any time.

What we do not collect

The game has no account system, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share personal data. We do not collect your location, contacts or photos. The game does not send any information about you to our servers. The only network traffic is the direct connection between players in an online match, as described above.

Permissions

The game may ask for access to the microphone (only for voice chat) and the network (online matches). You can refuse or withdraw the microphone permission in your device settings and the rest of the game will still work.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because online matches let players chat with strangers, parents should supervise younger players or keep them to offline games.

Deleting your data

Because the information is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings or uninstalling the game.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
