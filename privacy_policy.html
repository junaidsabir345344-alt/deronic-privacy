Deronic Throne - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Deronic Throne ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings (graphics, sound, controls), your player name, your profile progress, unlocked items and match statistics in a file on your own device. If you do not create an account, this information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Optional accounts

You can play without an account. If you create one, your email address and password are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which sends them over an encrypted connection. The developer never sees your password. A copy of your game progress (player name, match statistics, Glory rank, coins and cosmetics) is stored in Google Firebase and linked to your account so you can restore it on another device. This data is used only to run your account and is not sold or used for advertising.

You can delete your account at any time from the ACCOUNT screen in the game (Delete my account), or by emailing the address below from your account's email address. This permanently removes your email address, sign-in details and cloud save. Instructions are on the account deletion page: Delete your account

Online matches with friends

When you choose to play online, one player hosts the match and the other players connect to that player's device directly. During the match the game sends what is needed to play together: your player name, your hero's moves and actions, text chat messages and pings. If you use voice chat, your voice is sent live to your teammates in that match only. None of this is recorded or stored by the game or by the developer. To connect, the devices see each other's network (IP) address, as with any direct online connection.

Microphone

The microphone permission is only used for the optional team voice chat, and only while you hold (or switch on) the talk button in an online match. The game never records or saves audio.

No ads, no tracking

The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for your location, contacts or photos.

Purchases

The game offers an optional paid hero. Payments are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer never sees your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what you own.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not intended for them. Online play and voice chat are only used if a player chooses to join a match with friends.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
