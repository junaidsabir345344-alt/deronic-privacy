Delete your Tiny Warriors In Deronic Arena account

In the game

  1. Open Tiny Warriors and tap MORE at the bottom.
  2. Under ACCOUNT, tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap DELETE PERMANENTLY.

By email

If you no longer have the game, email jaayfee06@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the email address or player name of the account. It will be deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in details, your email address (if you added one), your cloud save and your World Top 3 score are permanently deleted. Progress saved on your own phone stays until you uninstall the game. Google Play and Google AdMob keep their own records under Google's terms.
