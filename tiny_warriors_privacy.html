Tiny Warriors In Deronic Arena - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tiny Warriors In Deronic Arena ("the game") is made by an independent developer, Junaid Sabir. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The developer does not sell your information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (heroes, cards, coins, gems, tickets, trophies, quests and settings) in a file on your own device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Your account and cloud save

When the game starts, it creates an anonymous guest account with Google Firebase Authentication, so your progress can be saved online. A guest account contains no name, email or contact details. You can choose to add an email address and password so you can sign in on another phone. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase; the developer never sees them.

A copy of your game progress and the player name you choose are stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database, servers in Singapore) and linked to your account. They are used only to run your account, restore your progress and run online play.

Online battles and the World Top 3

When you look for a ranked battle, the game shares your player name, trophy count, hero team and, during the battle, your board, hearts and the monsters you send, with the other player. This is stored only while the match is running. Your player name and your best number of waves cleared are shown to other players in the World Top 3 list. If no player is found quickly, you may be matched against a computer opponent.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob: you choose to watch an ad to get a reward. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The game sells optional gem packs. Payments are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer never sees your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought.

What the game does not do

The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and it does not use any other analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game: More (Settings) → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your sign-in details, email address, cloud save and World Top 3 score. Full instructions, including how to ask by email: Delete your account

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
